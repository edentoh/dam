Image upload guidelines (Draw-A-Man)

  • Upload a single child’s drawing of a person (not a photo of a person, not a document/worksheet).
  • Use a plain sheet of paper on a flat surface; avoid patterned backgrounds.
  • Ensure the full page is visible, well-lit, and captured straight-on (minimise shadows and glare).
  • Avoid extreme tilt/rotation; keep the drawing upright if possible.
  • Do not upload blank pages; include the complete drawing in-frame.
Note: The system applies automatic filtering and may reject non-DAM images (photos, documents, blank pages, very low quality captures).
